NarcoScope

NarcoScope is a research interface for aggregate public drug-market data. It covers retail prices, seizures, precursor flows, overdose mortality, and sanctions designations from UNODC, INCB, EUDA, CDC, and the US Treasury.

Every view identifies its source and data state. Official extracts and illustrative records are labelled separately. The project does not provide point-of-sale, real-time, or navigable location data.

Read the source and methodology on GitHub.
