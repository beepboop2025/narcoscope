Lawful industrial trade: the missing denominator

GACC publishes official merchandise-trade statistics, while UN Comtrade exposes aggregate trade by reporter, partner, product and period. [1]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12[2]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12

NarcoScope has not admitted a chemical-product series from either capability into this dossier, so lawful trade is not quantified here. [1]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12[2]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12

official incidents

What the INCB report actually says

INCB reported that, during the first ten months of 2025, 9 incidents involving nearly 5 tonnes of two related methamphetamine pre-precursors were communicated through PICS. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26

The report said those substances were mislabelled, reportedly originated in China and were destined for countries in the European Union. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26

That figure is a less-than multi-incident aggregate, not an exact 5,000 kg shipment, and NarcoScope does not add it to other qualified quantities. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26

For Operation Pseudonym, INCB said four participating countries reported 168 seizures, most in Australia and New Zealand, and reported China and India as origins in those two countries. [3]paragraph 46; PDF page 31; printed page 13

The public report does not allocate those seizures or their mass by origin-and-destination pair; Australia’s and New Zealand’s annual totals are separate aggregates and are excluded here. [3]paragraph 46; PDF page 31; printed page 13[3]paragraphs 67 and 71–72; printed pages 19–20

harm

The US harm trend is real—and separate

Across every retained December checkpoint, CDC’s provisional US count involving synthetic opioids excluding methadone rose from 9,610 in 2015 to a peak of 74,829 in 2022, then fell 48.6% to 38,458 in 2025. [6]United States; 12 month-ending December; synthetic_opioids (T40.4 excluding methadone); 2015–2025

The category is “synthetic opioids excluding methadone (T40.4),” not all synthetic opioids and not a measure of precursor supply. [6]United States; 12 month-ending December; synthetic_opioids (T40.4 excluding methadone); 2015–2025

The CDC rows contain no exporter, shipment, precursor or country-of-origin field, so the series is harm context rather than corroboration of the INCB incidents. what cannot show

The evidence gap is the finding

The official record supports one attributed finding: INCB reported a China-origin aggregate of nearly five tonnes across nine incidents bound for European Union countries. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26

It supports zero independently corroborated event claims because no second admitted source confirms the same incident set at compatible grain. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26[6]United States; 12 month-ending December; synthetic_opioids (T40.4 excluding methadone); 2015–2025

The available sources do not establish a record-level causal chain from lawful production or export to an incident, an organization or a death. [1]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12[2]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12[3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26[6]United States; 12 month-ending December; synthetic_opioids (T40.4 excluding methadone); 2015–2025

The defensible automated conclusion is therefore narrower than a national accusation: the report identifies a pattern worth investigating and the public data needed to test its consequences are missing. countercase

Countercase and evidence still needed

The INCB aggregate and the separately declining CDC series are compatible with many explanations, including enforcement, reporting, market and treatment changes; this build cannot choose among them. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26[6]United States; 12 month-ending December; synthetic_opioids (T40.4 excluding methadone); 2015–2025

A stronger test would require a lawful-trade denominator, shipment-level regulatory notifications, incident identifiers and adjudicated outcomes with defensible shared keys. limitations

Limitations

  • The INCB extract covers selected reported incidents and cannot estimate all precursor trade, diversion or successful movement. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26
  • Nearly five tonnes preserves the report’s less-than precision and must not be represented as one exact shipment. [3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26
  • No admitted GACC or UN Comtrade chemical-product series supplies a lawful industrial-trade denominator. [1]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12[2]registered capability and access boundary; as of 2026-08-12
  • Operation Pseudonym names China and India as reported origins but does not publish bilateral count or mass allocations in paragraph 46. [3]paragraph 46; PDF page 31; printed page 13
  • CDC T40.4 mortality is provisional and has no record-level join to the INCB incidents. [6]United States; 12 month-ending December; synthetic_opioids (T40.4 excluding methadone); 2015–2025[3]paragraph 94; PDF page 44; printed page 26
  • Restricted PICS and PEN Online data and automated federal docket review are outside this public offline build. [4]registered restricted-system capability; as of 2026-08-12[5]registered restricted-system capability; as of 2026-08-12[7]registered account-and-fee access boundary; as of 2026-08-12
