The safety boundary is part of the API.

NarcoScope exposes aggregate published evidence—not point-of-sale, real-time, sub-street, navigable, synthesis-route, chemistry, or yield information. A seizure is an administrative observation, not a direct market-size estimate. An origin label or public designation is not guilt or causal proof. The corridor artifact permits geography-and-time comparison only; it cannot classify a political movement, armed organization, community, person, or bilateral route.
